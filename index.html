Tools
Python, Tableau

RFM Customer Segmentation, Online Retail

Problem
Which customers generate the most revenue, and which are worth winning back?
Tools
SQL (CTEs, window functions), Python, Tableau
Result
  • Cleaned about 1.07M transactions to about 777K valid records (5,852 customers) and scored them into six segments.
  • Found that 27% of customers (Champions) generate 71% of £16.8M revenue.
  • Flagged 1,206 At Risk customers (about £1.76M past spend) as the main win-back opportunity.
